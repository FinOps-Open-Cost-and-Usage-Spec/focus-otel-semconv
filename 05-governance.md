# Governance Policy

This Working Group follows the **Community Specification Governance Policy**. The authoritative text is published by the Community Specification project:

- [Community Specification Governance Policy, V1.1](https://github.com/CommunitySpecification/Community_Specification/blob/V1.1/5._Governance.md)

This repository does not keep a copy of that policy, so there is only one authoritative version and no risk of the two drifting apart. Section references in this repository, such as "Governance §2.1", refer to that document.

The Working Group operates under the FOCUS Semantic Conventions for OpenTelemetry Working Group Charter and remains subject to the governance authority of the FOCUS Steering Committee. See [Project governance](README.md#project-governance).
